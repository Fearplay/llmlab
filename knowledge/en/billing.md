# Billing, Invoices, and Refund Settlement

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Payment and invoices

Atlas Works accepts the methods presented at checkout. Payment authorisation may occur when an order is placed, while capture may occur when fulfilment begins. Enterprise invoice terms require written approval and are shown on the account; support agents cannot create payment terms in chat.

An invoice lists the seller entity, purchaser details, order lines, discounts, shipping, and applicable tax treatment. Customers request a correction through Billing within 30 calendar days of invoice issue. A correction changes clerical information where permitted but does not transfer a completed purchase between unrelated legal entities.

## Failed and duplicate charges

A pending bank authorisation is not necessarily a settled charge. Customers should compare the merchant reference and settlement status before reporting a duplicate. Billing investigates two settled charges with the same order reference within three business days. A chargeback can pause a parallel refund to avoid duplicate settlement.

## Refund settlement

The eligibility decision comes from [[ATLAS-RETURNS-001]] or [[ATLAS-SUBSCRIPTIONS-001]]. After approval, Billing submits the refund to the original payment route. The returns policy defines the five-business-day internal processing target. Card issuers and banks control final posting time.

Credit notes reference the original invoice and show line-level amounts. Store credit is used only when the original route cannot accept a refund and the customer agrees. Atlas Works never asks a customer to pay a fee, buy a gift card, or disclose a one-time password to release a refund.

## Taxes and currency

Displayed tax depends on the supplied billing and delivery information. A tax exemption must be validated before invoice finalisation unless local rules permit later correction. Currency conversion performed by a card issuer is outside Atlas Works control. The order currency remains the refund currency even when the customer's bank displays a different home-currency amount.
