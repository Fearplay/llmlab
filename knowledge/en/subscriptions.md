# Atlas Cloud Subscription Policy

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Plans and billing periods

Atlas Cloud Core provides device inventory and basic alerts. Atlas Cloud Fleet adds remote configuration and staged firmware rollout. Atlas Cloud Audit adds extended event retention and export controls. A workspace has one primary plan, though separately priced capacity packs may be attached.

Monthly subscriptions renew each month on the activation day. Annual subscriptions renew each year on the activation date. If a month lacks that day, renewal occurs on its final day. Renewal charges use the saved payment method unless approved invoice terms apply.

## Cancellation

A workspace owner may cancel in the Admin Console before the renewal timestamp. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues through the paid period. Ordinary mid-period cancellation does not create a prorated refund. A duplicate subscription or verified service-billing error should be reported to Billing within 30 calendar days.

An annual plan may be changed to a lower tier at the next renewal. An upgrade takes effect immediately and the checkout preview shows any prorated amount. Removing a capacity pack may require reducing managed devices before the next renewal.

## Trials and suspension

A trial lasts 14 calendar days unless the invitation states another duration. Only one standard trial is available per workspace. A trial can end without a charge when no payment method was submitted. When a payment method was submitted for conversion, the Admin Console shows the conversion date before confirmation.

Atlas Works may suspend access after a failed renewal and two unsuccessful retry attempts over seven calendar days. Data is retained for 30 calendar days after suspension, then scheduled for deletion under [[ATLAS-PRIVACY-001]]. Security or abuse suspension may occur immediately under [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]].

## Export before closure

Workspace owners should export required configuration and audit data before the paid period ends. Support cannot restore every data type after deletion begins. Hardware returns are separate and follow [[ATLAS-RETURNS-001]].
