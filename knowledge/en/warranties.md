# Limited Hardware Warranty

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Coverage period

Atlas Hub, Atlas Display, and Atlas Dock include a 24-month limited warranty from delivery. Atlas Sensor includes an 18-month limited warranty because its field environment differs. A replacement unit inherits the later of 90 calendar days from replacement delivery or the remaining original warranty period.

The warranty covers a manufacturing defect that prevents normal operation under the published technical limits. It does not cover cosmetic wear, consumable adhesive pads, batteries after their stated cycle life, misuse, liquid ingress, unauthorised repair, unsupported power equipment, or operation outside the environmental range in [[ATLAS-OPS-001]].

## Claim process

The account owner opens a warranty case with the order number, product name, final six serial characters, firmware version, and a concise symptom description. Support may request safe diagnostic logs. Customers should remove personal data and local credentials before returning a unit. Security-sensitive logs use the protected upload path in [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]].

Support first checks configuration and known issues. If a hardware defect is probable, Support issues an RMA. Atlas Works may repair the unit, replace it with the same model or an equivalent model, or refund the depreciated purchase price when repair and replacement are not reasonable. A customer cannot select a newer product as the default remedy.

## Advance replacement

Enterprise customers with Atlas Care Plus may receive advance replacement after a payment hold or contract verification. The failed unit must be scanned by the return carrier within ten calendar days after the replacement arrives. If it is not scanned, the account may be billed for the replacement. This return is a warranty logistics step, not the standard return policy in [[ATLAS-RETURNS-001]].

## Data responsibility

Warranty service does not guarantee preservation of device configuration or locally stored logs. Customers maintain their own exports. Atlas Works technicians follow access controls and media handling in [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]], but a service event may require factory reset.
