# Privacy and Data Handling Notice

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Data categories and purpose

Atlas Works processes account identity, workspace membership, billing references, support correspondence, device inventory, configuration history, and service telemetry. Atlas Sensor reports occupancy state and environmental measurements; the standard product does not record speech or store raw audio. Diagnostic logs may contain network identifiers and must be treated as confidential operational data.

Data is used to deliver the service, secure accounts, process orders, provide support, maintain reliability, and meet documented business obligations. Atlas Works does not claim that this synthetic notice satisfies any real jurisdiction. Demonstration users must not upload real personal or confidential information to the sample environment.

## Retention

Active workspace configuration is retained while the subscription is active. After a billing suspension, recoverable workspace data is retained for 30 calendar days as described in [[ATLAS-SUBSCRIPTIONS-001]]. Standard security events are retained for 90 days; Atlas Cloud Audit customers may configure up to 365 days. Billing records may be retained longer than workspace content where required by the fictional accounting schedule.

Support attachments are deleted 60 days after the case closes unless they are linked to an active security investigation, warranty process, or legal hold. Agents should request only the minimum diagnostic material required and should not copy attachments into unrelated systems.

## Requests and deletion

An authenticated workspace Owner can request export or deletion through the Privacy page. Identity verification is required before fulfilment. Atlas Works acknowledges a request within three business days and normally completes it within 30 calendar days. A request may exclude data that must be retained for an unresolved payment dispute, active incident, or documented record obligation.

Deleting a user account removes direct access but does not rewrite attributable audit history. Audit records may replace a display name with a stable pseudonymous identifier. Account security controls are in [[ATLAS-ACCOUNTS-001]], and operational safeguards are in [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]].
