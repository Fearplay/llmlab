# Shipping and Delivery Policy

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Dispatch targets

In-stock standard hardware normally leaves the fulfilment centre within two business days after payment approval. Configured hardware normally leaves within seven business days. Custom hardware follows the accepted project schedule and has no default dispatch target. These are dispatch targets, not guaranteed arrival dates.

Standard delivery in supported mainland regions is estimated at three to five business days after dispatch. Priority delivery is estimated at one to two business days. Remote areas, customs review, severe weather, and carrier restrictions can extend transit. An order is not considered lost until the carrier has no scan for seven consecutive calendar days after the estimated delivery date.

## Address changes and split orders

An address may be changed before the fulfilment state becomes `packed`. After that state, Support can request a carrier redirect but cannot guarantee it. A redirect never changes the purchaser or tax jurisdiction on the invoice. Orders containing products with different lead times may ship in separate parcels at no extra standard-shipping charge.

## Delivery evidence

Orders worth USD 750 or more require a signature unless an enterprise delivery agreement states otherwise. The carrier scan is the initial evidence of delivery. A customer disputing delivery should open a case within five calendar days of the scan and include the order number, delivery address confirmation, and any carrier notice. Atlas Works reviews the carrier record before sending a replacement.

Damage visible at delivery should be photographed before the package is discarded. Transit-damage claims opened within five calendar days use the damaged-delivery route in [[ATLAS-RETURNS-001]] and do not consume the ordinary return window. Warranty defects discovered after successful delivery follow [[ATLAS-WARRANTY-001]].

## Cross-border shipments

The checkout page identifies whether duties are prepaid. When duties are not prepaid, the recipient is responsible for import assessment. Refusing a parcel to avoid duties is not an approved return; documented carrier and handling costs may be deducted from a refund. Billing corrections are described in [[ATLAS-BILLING-001]].
