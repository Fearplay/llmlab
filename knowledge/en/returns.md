# Returns and Refunds Policy

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Standard returns

<!-- rule:returns.standard_days=21 -->
Standard hardware can be returned within 21 calendar days of delivery. The item must be in resalable condition and include its assigned power supply, mounting parts, and serialised components. Opening the package is allowed; normal inspection is allowed; damage caused by installation, drilling, liquid, or an unsupported power source is not normal inspection.

The customer must request a return material authorisation, or RMA, through the Help Center before shipping anything. The RMA is valid for 14 calendar days. A parcel sent without an RMA may be delayed or returned to the sender. The ordinary return window is measured from recorded delivery to the RMA request, not from warehouse receipt.

Configured hardware, defined in [[ATLAS-PRODUCTS-001]], may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery. Custom hardware, engraved units, delivered professional services, activated prepaid support packs, and gift cards are not returnable unless the delivered item is damaged or materially different from the approved order.

## Damaged or incorrect delivery

Transit damage or an incorrect SKU should be reported within five calendar days of delivery. Atlas Works provides a prepaid label after Support verifies the order and evidence. A timely damaged-delivery case is handled separately from buyer-remorse eligibility. Customers should retain packaging until Support confirms that photographs are sufficient.

## Refund processing

<!-- rule:refunds.processing_days=5 -->
Approved refunds are processed within five business days after the warehouse completes inspection. Bank or card-network posting time is outside Atlas Works control and may add further days. Refunds go to the original payment method unless that method is legally or technically unavailable; Support cannot redirect a refund to an unrelated account.

Original standard shipping is refunded only when Atlas Works sent an incorrect item or verified transit damage. Priority surcharges are not refunded for buyer-remorse returns. A missing accessory may be deducted at its published replacement price, but the warehouse must record the deduction in the inspection result.

## Exchanges and exceptions

Atlas Works does not reserve exchange stock. Support creates a return and a separate replacement order; price differences are shown before confirmation. Policy Operations may approve an exception for a documented service failure. An exception must name the approver, scope, and expiry and does not amend this policy for other customers.

Subscription cancellations follow [[ATLAS-SUBSCRIPTIONS-001]]. Defective products outside the return window may qualify under [[ATLAS-WARRANTY-001]]. Delivery evidence and lost-parcel rules are in [[ATLAS-SHIPPING-001]].
