# Product Catalog and Classification

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Standard hardware

Standard hardware means an unmodified Atlas Hub, Atlas Sensor, Atlas Display, or Atlas Dock purchased from the normal catalog. Standard hardware uses the general return window in [[ATLAS-RETURNS-001]] and the limited hardware warranty in [[ATLAS-WARRANTY-001]]. Packaging may contain several components, but the product is treated as one return unit when sold under one serialised kit number.

Atlas Hub is the desk-side controller for up to 24 Atlas Sensors. Atlas Sensor measures occupancy and ambient conditions; it does not record audio. Atlas Display is the room-status panel. Atlas Dock supplies power and wired network connectivity to a Hub. The product name printed on the order controls classification when marketing labels differ.

## Configured and custom hardware

Configured hardware has customer-selected memory, mounting, colour, or firmware but uses an existing catalog base. It has a 14-calendar-day return window, not the standard window. Custom hardware is built from a customer drawing, engraved with a unique mark, or assigned a non-catalog electrical design. Custom hardware is not returnable unless it arrives damaged or materially differs from the approved specification.

Prototype kits, evaluation loaners, and replacement units are not retail purchases. A loaner remains Atlas Works property and follows the due date on its loan record. A warranty replacement inherits the remaining warranty of the original unit; it does not begin a new full term.

## Software and services

Atlas Cloud Core, Atlas Cloud Fleet, and Atlas Cloud Audit are subscription plans governed by [[ATLAS-SUBSCRIPTIONS-001]]. A perpetual firmware entitlement included with hardware is not a separate subscription. Professional installation, data migration, and on-site training are services. Once delivered, service hours are not hardware and cannot be returned through the warehouse process.

## Identification

Every shipped hardware unit has a serial number in the form `AWX-YYYY-NNNNNN`. Customers should never publish a full serial number in a public forum. Support may ask for the final six characters through an authenticated case. Shipping labels and invoices use the catalog SKU; the serial number identifies the physical unit.
