# Business Travel and Expense Policy

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Approval and booking

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. International travel also requires the cost centre owner. Employees use the approved booking service when available. Economy class is required for flights under six scheduled hours. Premium economy may be approved for a longer flight; business class requires an executive exception documented before purchase.

Travellers select a reasonable fare that meets the business schedule. Personal stopovers are allowed only when they do not increase company cost or operational risk. The traveller reimburses any incremental personal cost and clearly separates it from the business itinerary.

## Lodging, meals, and ground transport

Hotels should remain within the nightly city cap published in the booking tool. When conference lodging exceeds the cap, the manager may approve it if total transport and safety make it reasonable. Meals are reimbursed at actual reasonable cost up to USD 75 per day; alcohol is not reimbursable except at an approved customer event.

Public transport or a standard taxi is preferred. A rental car is appropriate when it reduces total cost, protects safety, or serves a location without practical transit. Traffic fines, minibar purchases, leisure entertainment, and upgrades for personal preference are not reimbursable.

## Submission

Expenses must be submitted within 20 calendar days after travel ends. A receipt is required for any single item of USD 25 or more. Missing receipts require a signed explanation and manager approval; repeated missing receipts are reviewed by Finance. The employee identifies business purpose, attendees for hosted meals, project, and currency.

Corporate-card charges still require an expense report. A card is not personal credit and must not be used for routine personal purchases. Information classification and device safety during travel follow [[ATLAS-EMPLOYEE-001]]. Suspected card fraud is reported to Finance and [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]] immediately.
