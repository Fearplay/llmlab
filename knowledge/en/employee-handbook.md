# Employee Handbook Summary

This is a synthetic demonstration document for the fictional organisation Atlas Works. It is not a real law, contract or commercial policy.

## Working principles

Employees protect customer trust, document decisions, seek review for high-impact changes, and raise concerns without retaliation. This synthetic handbook is an operational demonstration, not an employment contract. Local employment terms would control in a real organisation.

Managers define outcomes and reasonable working expectations. Employees record material project decisions in the approved workspace rather than private chat. Customer data is accessed only for a current work purpose and through an approved system. Curiosity is not a valid reason to inspect an account.

## Information handling

Company information is classified Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Credentials, private keys, vulnerability details, unreleased financial information, and raw customer exports are Restricted. Restricted data may not be pasted into unapproved AI tools, personal storage, or public issue trackers.

Devices use full-disk encryption, automatic screen locking, supported software, and centrally managed endpoint protection. A lost device is reported to the security on-call immediately, even when it appears powered off. Security reporting follows [[ATLAS-SECURITY-001]] and incident coordination follows [[ATLAS-INCIDENT-001]].

## Time away and expenses

Planned leave is recorded in the people system and handed over before absence. On-call responsibilities must be explicitly transferred. Business travel and reimbursable expenses follow [[ATLAS-TRAVEL-001]]; a manager's informal approval does not override receipt or timing requirements.

## Conflicts and reporting

Employees disclose outside interests that could affect purchasing, hiring, or customer decisions. Gifts over USD 75 from a current supplier require Ethics review. Good-faith concerns can be raised to a manager, People Operations, Security, or the confidential reporting channel. Retaliation for a good-faith report is prohibited.
